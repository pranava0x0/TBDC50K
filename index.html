Before you run

Check-in: opens 6:30 AM (final Nov 25 runner email); arrive early. Bring your first item before 8 AM, four bib pins, and a large wrapper bag. Bathrooms are limited.

Food: buying food at any Taco Bell in advance is allowed (first 1–2 recommended). Finish one item at each stop before moving on; sides count, drinks don’t. Save all nine wrappers/receipts.

Gear check: Made in ALX next door, 6 AM–8 PM; one closed bag, collect by noon Saturday.

Navigation: follow the stop order; paths between them are your choice. No aid stations. Use sidewalks/trails, bring water, phone battery backup, reflective vest and a headlamp if finishing after 5 PM.
